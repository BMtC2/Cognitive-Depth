The task is the wrong unit.
One analytical report asks a student to gather material, apply a method, interpret evidence and build an argument. Four kinds of thinking. A rule for the whole task cannot tell them apart, however detailed it is.

Two kinds of framework
Bloom tells you what to measure.
This one tells you where the machine stops.
Analytic
Bloom, SOLO
Decompose cognition into levels that can be specified and marked. Anything specified clearly enough to mark reliably is codifiable, and a pattern-based system can increasingly produce it without the thinking behind it.
Holistic
Four levels, four images
Each level is a whole mode of knowing, held in one image. The axis is codifiability, the one property that tracks the edge of a system built from patterns.
The two kinds complement each other. Nisbett et al. (2001) on holistic and analytic cognition; Biggs (1996) on constructive alignment.
AI capability is the inverse of cognitive depth.
Depth here is not difficulty or value. It is how far the work can be reduced to rules and patterns.
AI capability Codifiability
The break is structural.The missing input was never recorded, so more training cannot supply it.
1 Broad knowledge
Near-complete coverage, not self-verifying
2 Analogical transfer
Substantial but unreliable at the margin
3 Contextual inference
Partial at best; degrades sharply
4 Original creation
Beyond current and near-term capability

"We know more than we can tell." Polanyi (1966). A system trained on what was recorded has no access to what never was.
The failure mode
Plausible, well expressed, and wrong
in ways only Level 3 judgement can detect.
Extension
A tool used by someone who already has the capability. They can see what the model missed.
Substitution
A tool used in place of acquiring the capability. The only check is the judgement they delegated.
Cognitive offloading: Risko & Gilbert (2016); Zhai et al. (2024); Gerlich (2025); Fan et al. (2025).
The deeper the work,
the more the design protects it.
And the more legitimate it becomes to delegate the shallower work around it. Within one task, not only across tasks.

Scope
What this paper does not claim
A measurement taxonomy
It does not grade work or sequence curriculum. Bloom and SOLO keep their jobs. This is a lens for design judgement.
A fixed prediction
The mapping is for current and near-term systems. The Level 3 argument is structural, but it should be revisited as systems change.
An empirical finding at Level 4
"Generates but does not originate" is a philosophical position, open to challenge on those terms.
Evidence of better learning
The cases are illustrative. Two questions are testable, and the evaluation is planned across the core courses of one undergraduate program.
